## Project Overview
### Copper Bus Bar Problem
The objective of this study was to examine the electrothermal behavior of a copper bus bar under a specified temperature constraint. Specifically, the goal was to determine the maximum voltage that could be applied across the bus bar without its temperature exceeding 350 K.
### Unit Circle Problem
The objective of this study was to investigate the effect of material selection on the thermal behavior of a unit circle. The analysis compared the resulting temperature profiles for three different materials: gold, lead, and helium gas.
