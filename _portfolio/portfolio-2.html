Project 1: Controlling Three LEDs with a Potentiometer

Created a 3-LED system that is powered based on the analog pin reading associated with the potentiometer (0–1023).

Demonstration of a 3-LED system responding to potentiometer inputs

Project 2: Controlling LED Brightness with Push Buttons and a Passive Buzzer

Controlled LED brightness using two push buttons to increment or decrement the PWM output. A passive buzzer provided an audible alert when the brightness reached its upper or lower limit (0 or 255).

Demonstration of LED brightness being controlled with push buttons. A passive buzzer is sounded when the brightness reaches a limit

Project 3: Controlling Servo Motor with a Photoresistor

Controlled a servo horn from 0° to 45° by mapping the photoresistor's analog reading (0–800) to the servo angle, with 800 representing the maximum light level measured under the test conditions.

Demonstration of a servo motor being controlled by a photoresistor. The light is obstrcuted when the resistor is covered
