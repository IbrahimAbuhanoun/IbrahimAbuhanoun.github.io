## Gas Turbine Design Project
The objective of this project was to use GasTurb 14 to model and analyze two-spool and three-spool turbofan engine configurations through multiple design iterations. The engine cycles were designed to achieve a takeoff net thrust of 150 kN while maintaining a specific fuel consumption (SFC) of approximately 11.33 g/(kN·s) or lower if possible.
